The year filter Changes only what you are looking at. A sale is filed under the year of its own date and that can never be set by hand, so nothing can be filed into the wrong year by leaving this on an earlier one. The worklist and the failures ignore it entirely — anything still open shows regardless of when it started.

Two things missing here Rows are listed one per recharge, not grouped under their payment, and there is no “paid by” column: the history endpoint returns recharges without a payment id or settlement lines. And voiding a completed sale has no button because there is no longer anything to press: the endpoint was withdrawn, not left unwired. A void could not hand the money back — it returned it to the held account and nothing could clear it from there — so a delivered sale that should be undone is marked failed and then given back, both of which have buttons and both of which post the same reversal.
